# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

HyreEdge Enterprise AI is an intelligent workspace that combines Technology, Legal, Research, Document Analysis and AI-powered assistance into a single platform.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Professionals often use multiple tools for coding, legal drafting, document analysis, research and AI conversations.

HyreEdge Enterprise AI brings these capabilities into one unified workspace.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Save users time
- Increase productivity
- Provide expert-level assistance
- Deliver a premium user experience
- Support both technical and legal workflows

---

## 4. Target Audience

- Developers
- Students
- Lawyers
- Startups
- Businesses
- Researchers
